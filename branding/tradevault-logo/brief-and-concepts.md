### Strictly Forbidden Clichés (Category Ban List)
- Literal financial candlesticks, wicks, or OHLC bars
- Upward-pointing stock arrows, swoosh trends, or rocket motifs
- Generic security shields, keyholes, padlocks, or bank columns
- Checkmarks, generic hexagons, or isometric 3D cubes
- Decorative gradients, neon glow, drop shadows, or translucent overlaps
- Close imitation of MEXC (overlapping twin mountain chevrons/peaks) or any exchange logo

## Phase 3: Ideation — 10 Distinct Written Concept Directions

1. **Concept 1 — "Cadence Pillars" (Modular Rhythm / Negative-Space Equilibrium)** *(Abstract / Modular)*
   Three wide, softly rounded vertical pillars arranged on a strict modular grid where the outer pillars anchor the left and right edges while the split central column creates a calm negative-space "rest interval" at the optical center, subtly echoing a `T` crossbar and `V` valley through pure vertical cadence.
2. **Concept 2 — "The Breathing Vault" (Offset Interlocking Vault Arcs)** *(Abstract / Negative Space)*
   Two thick, rounded U-shaped vault arches—one descending from the top-left, one ascending from the bottom-right—interlock without touching so the negative space between them carves a calm, continuous equilibrium wave through a rounded-square footprint.
3. **Concept 3 — "Counterpoint Monolith" (Carved Squircle with Stepped T–V Chamber)** *(Negative-Space / Letterform Abstraction)*
   A solid rounded-square vault stone pierced by a single continuous, softly radiused negative-space channel that spans horizontally across the upper tier (`T` horizon) before stepping down into a centered lower vessel (`V` chamber).
4. **Concept 4 — "Equilibrium Pendulum" (Circle & Counter-Arc Balance)** *(Abstract / Geometric)*
   A solid circular weight poised above a thick, wide crescent cradle within a 256×256 frame, expressing emotional equilibrium and risk control through two fundamental primitives separated by a constant 24-unit air gap.
5. **Concept 5 — "Metronome Ledger" (Horizontal Cadence Steps)** *(Abstract / Grid-Modular)*
   Three thick horizontal stadium bars stacked vertically with rhythmic left-to-right indentation (top full-width like a `T` cap, middle indented right, bottom centered like a `V` base), evoking a calm journal ledger and measured tempo.
6. **Concept 6 — "Keystone Aperture" (Four-Quadrant Breathing Vault)** *(Negative-Space / Containment)*
   Four softly rounded corner blocks arranged in a square grid whose inward-facing curves carve an asymmetrical, smooth diamond-circle "breathing aperture" at the center of the mark.
7. **Concept 7 — "Continuous Ribbon Loop" (Single-Path Mobius Cadence)** *(Abstract / Monoline-to-Outline)*
   A continuous thick stadium track that folds inward at 90-degree rounded turns to form an asymmetric two-chamber loop representing the cycle of trade execution and post-trade review.
8. **Concept 8 — "Twin Vessel Monogram" (Joined `TV` Negative-Space Ligature)** *(Letterform / Monogram)*
   A heavy geometric `T` and `V` fused into a single rounded block where the right arm of the `T` seamlessly becomes the left stem of the `V`, leaving a rounded triangular negative-space notch at the top right.
9. **Concept 9 — "Acoustic Horizon" (Split Disc with Offset Wave Cutout)** *(Abstract / Circle)*
   A solid circular disc divided horizontally by a stepped, rounded negative-space channel (high on the left, low on the right) that balances upper and lower hemispheres in a 1:1 surface-area ratio.
10. **Concept 10 — "The Measured Wordmark" (Custom `TradeVault` Type with Integrated Rhythm Cut)** *(Wordmark / Custom Type)*
    A standalone custom geometric wordmark where the crossbar of `T` and the counter of `V` share the exact same rounded capsule geometry and negative-space gap, turning the name itself into the rhythmic lockup.

### Why These Three Concepts Were Selected
- **Genuinely Distinct Visual Premises**:
  - **Concept A (*Cadence Pillars*)** explores **vertical modular rhythm** (open silhouette, repeated rounded pillars + central rest interval).
  - **Concept B (*The Breathing Vault*)** explores **rotational interlocking containment** (two offset rounded vault U-arches forming a fluid S-cadence channel).
  - **Concept C (*Counterpoint Monolith*)** explores **figure-ground negative-space carving** inside a single solid squircle container (`T` horizon + `V` vessel chamber).
- **Zero Category Clichés**: None of the three use candlesticks, arrows, shields, padlocks, hexagons, or MEXC-style twin chevrons.
- **16px Durability**: Every stroke, pillar, and negative-space channel measures `>= 20–44` units on a `256×256` canvas (`>= 1.25px–2.75px` at `16×16px` favicon size).
