Jurnal Trading XAUUSD, Analitik Performa, & Manajemen Risiko

XAUUSD Trading Journal, Performance Analytics & Risk Workspace

Vlo Journal adalah aplikasi pencatatan transaksi dan evaluasi disiplin trading yang membantu trader memantau ekuitas, menghitung ukuran posisi (lot) secara presisi, meninjau berita makro serta siklus pasar, dan menyinkronkan jurnal lintas perangkat secara aman melalui VLO Cloud.

Vlo Journal is a structured trade logging and execution review application built to help traders track equity curves, calculate position sizes accurately, monitor macro events and market cycles, and securely synchronize journals across devices via VLO Cloud.

Jurnal & Audit Eksekusi

Trade Log & Execution Audit

Catat entri, stop loss, take profit, komisi, swap, serta screenshot sebelum dan sesudah transaksi dalam satu ruang kerja terstruktur.

Record entry, stop loss, take profit, commission, swap, and before/after trade chart screenshots in one organized journal.

Analitik & Kalkulator Risiko

Analytics & Risk Sizing

Pantau Win Rate, Profit Factor, Expectancy, Drawdown, batas kerugian harian, dan simulasi risiko secara real-time.

Monitor Win Rate, Profit Factor, Expectancy, Drawdown, daily loss limits, and position risk simulations in real time.

Mode Cloud & Mode Lokal

Cloud Sync & Local Mode

Masuk dengan Google atau Akun VLO untuk sinkronisasi cloud, atau gunakan Mode Lokal di perangkat tanpa akun.

Sign in with Google or a VLO Account for encrypted cloud sync, or use Local Mode directly on your device.

Privasi & Isolasi Data Pengguna (RLS)

User Data Privacy & Row-Level Security

Setiap akun pengguna memiliki isolasi data ketat berbasis Row Level Security (RLS) dan folder penyimpanan privat terenkripsi. Informasi profil OAuth hanya digunakan untuk autentikasi sesi login.

Every user account is strictly isolated using database Row Level Security (RLS) and private storage buckets. OAuth profile information is used solely for authenticating your Vlo Journal account.
